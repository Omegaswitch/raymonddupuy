# Translation decisions

Status key: provisional = needs expert confirmation; settled = editorial decision for this edition.

## D-001 Warden → „strážce“
- Passages: rule-preamble-p001. Source: "Warden of the Hospital of Jerusalem". Latin *custos*, Old French *garde de l'Ospital* (Cart. I no. 70, opened 2026-11-06; research/references/002). Alternatives: správce, ochránce. Reason: closest calque of *custos*/*warden*; avoids "mistr" because Raymond does not use it of himself. Status: provisional (meaning verified; Czech word unattested).

## D-002 modesty → „cudnost“
- rule-s04-p001. Distinguishes *modesty* from *chastity* (čistota, §1). Alt: slušnost. Latin *pudicitia* (Cart. I no. 70) supports „cudnost“. Provisional.

## D-003 "buy by measure one meal only" → „koupí odměřeně jediné jídlo“
- rule-s05-p001. Ambiguous English; kept ambiguity. French *amesureement une soule viande*, Latin *mensurate emant unum solum cibum* = "buy with measure a single food/dish" (Cart. I no. 70); „odměřeně“ is defensible, „s mírou“ would be closer. Provisional.

## D-004 security → „zástava“
- rule-s06-p001. Alt: záruka. French *gage*, Latin *pignus* (Cart. I no. 70) = pledge → „zástava“ fits. Provisional.

## D-005 Master of the Church → „Mistr kostela“
- rule-s07-p001. King: presumably "Prior of the Church"; Riley-Smith 2012 p. 26: head of priests. Attested in both medieval texts: Fr. *le maistre de l'iglise*, Lat. *magistri ecclesie* (Cart. I no. 70; Latin case/number to be checked on the page image). "Církev" rejected as misleading. Provisional.

## D-006 ysambruns/galembruns kept in French
- rule-s08-p001. AND² (opened 2026-11-06): *isembrun* "dark-coloured cloth", *galebrun* "type of cloth" (research/references/003 V1); Latin *pannos ysambrunos et galambrunos*. King's gloss "brightly coloured" is therefore doubtful. Body keeps King's wording (fidelity to the source); translator's note gives the dictionary meaning. Provisional (specialist confirmation of the cloth terms still wanted).

## D-007 pennes sauvages
- rule-s08-p001. King: "furs of animals"; French *sauvages* = wild; Latin *pelles silvestres* (Cart. I no. 70) confirms "wild". Followed King; note gives the Latin. Provisional.

## D-008 Procurator of the House → „prokurátor domu“
- rule-s10-p001. Alt: správce domu. Fr. *procureor de la maison*, Lat. *procurator domus* (Cart. I no. 70). Provisional (Czech word unattested).

## D-009 vengeance / justice / serjeants (§12)
- rule-s12-p001. "odplata", "soud (justice)", "sergenti". Fr. *sergens* / Lat. *servientes*; *venjance* / *vindictam accipiant*; *justise de la maison* / *justitia domus* (Cart. I no. 70). Provisional.

## D-010 charity (§14) → „milodar“; office → „obřad“
- rule-s14-p001. Alt: almužna, oficium. Provisional.

## D-011 §16 formula and syntax
- rule-s16-p001. "naši Páni nemocní" (calque; the French rubric has only *les seignors malades*, "our" is King's); participial English rendered with finite verbs (flagged); "Nejsvětější svátost" = Eucharist, confirmed by Fr. *cumenié* / Lat. *communicetur*; Latin names only the Master, not the Chapter (Cart. I no. 70). Provisional.

## D-012 chape → „kutna“
- rule-s19-p001. King "cassock"; *chape* is a hooded cloak; Latin *in cappis et in mantellis* (Cart. I no. 70). Czech equivalent of *cappa* is „kápě“; body keeps „kutna“ because it translates King's *cassock*, note points to „kápě“. Provisional.

## D-013 Czech form of the name
- All. "Raymond du Puy" kept in French form, declined in Czech ("Raymonda du Puy"). Czech Wikipedia uses the same form ("bl. Raymond du Puy … druhý velmistr johanitů"; "Nástupce Raymond du Puy" on the Gérard page; opened 2026-11-06, research/references/003 V6); no Czech scholarly monograph opened. Settled for this edition.

## D-014 Capitalisation
- Mistr, Špitál, Kapitula? Lowercase "kapitula", "dům", "obedience"; "Mistr" (capital) for the Master, "mistři" for the masters, mirroring King. Settled.

## D-015 King's "Blessed" in title
- King's heading "Blessed Raymond du Puy" is not reproduced in our title ("Řehole Raymonda du Puy"); recorded in the editorial statement. Settled.

## D-016 Textual divergences King vs. French/Latin (noted, not corrected)
- rule-s01-p001 (Latin adds *per manum sacerdotis et per librum*; *in ultimo certamine*), rule-s09-p001 (French *ou* vs Latin *ac*; King follows Latin), rule-s11-p001 (Latin adds *nisi puram aquam*), rule-s16-p001 (Latin: Master only). Policy: the body translates King; each divergence gets a translator's note citing Cart. I no. 70. Source: research/references/002-cartulaire-i-70-comparison.md (OCR text, page images not checked). Settled.

## D-017 Biblical echoes
- rule-s04-p001: *qui habitat in sanctis* is treated as an echo, not a citation (nearest: Vulg. Ps 21:4); rule-s11-p001: the Latin Rule reproduces Vulg. 2 Thess 3:12 *cum silentio … panem manducent*; the Czech Bible versions lose "silence" (Kralická „pokojně“, ČSP „s tichostí“), which the note states. Settled.
