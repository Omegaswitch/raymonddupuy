# Translation decisions

Status key: provisional = needs expert confirmation; settled = editorial decision for this edition.

## D-001 Warden → „strážce“
- Passages: rule-preamble-p001. Source: "Warden of the Hospital of Jerusalem". Latin opening *custos* (secondary source, ldysinger). Alternatives: správce, ochránce. Reason: closest calque of *custos*/*warden*; avoids "mistr" because Raymond does not use it of himself. Status: provisional.

## D-002 modesty → „cudnost“
- rule-s04-p001. Distinguishes *modesty* from *chastity* (čistota, §1). Alt: slušnost. Source term unseen. Provisional.

## D-003 "buy by measure one meal only" → „koupí odměřeně jediné jídlo“
- rule-s05-p001. Ambiguous English; kept ambiguity. Provisional.

## D-004 security → „zástava“
- rule-s06-p001. Alt: záruka. Provisional.

## D-005 Master of the Church → „Mistr kostela“
- rule-s07-p001. King: presumably "Prior of the Church"; Riley-Smith 2012 p. 26: head of priests. "Církev" rejected as misleading. Provisional.

## D-006 ysambruns/galembruns kept in French
- rule-s08-p001. Meaning unverified; King's gloss "brightly coloured cloth" translated, French retained. Provisional.

## D-007 pennes sauvages
- rule-s08-p001. King: "furs of animals"; French *sauvages* = wild. Followed King; note flags it. Provisional.

## D-008 Procurator of the House → „prokurátor domu“
- rule-s10-p001. Alt: správce domu. Provisional.

## D-009 vengeance / justice / serjeants (§12)
- rule-s12-p001. "odplata", "soud (justice)", "sergenti". Provisional.

## D-010 charity (§14) → „milodar“; office → „obřad“
- rule-s14-p001. Alt: almužna, oficium. Provisional.

## D-011 §16 formula and syntax
- rule-s16-p001. "naši Páni nemocní" (calque); participial English rendered with finite verbs (flagged); "Nejsvětější svátost" = Eucharist. Provisional.

## D-012 chape → „kutna“
- rule-s19-p001. King "cassock"; *chape* is a hooded cloak. Provisional.

## D-013 Czech form of the name
- All. "Raymond du Puy" kept in French form, declined in Czech ("Raymonda du Puy"); no Czech scholarly form found. Settled for this edition.

## D-014 Capitalisation
- Mistr, Špitál, Kapitula? Lowercase "kapitula", "dům", "obedience"; "Mistr" (capital) for the Master, "mistři" for the masters, mirroring King. Settled.

## D-015 King's "Blessed" in title
- King's heading "Blessed Raymond du Puy" is not reproduced in our title ("Řehole Raymonda du Puy"); recorded in the editorial statement. Settled.
